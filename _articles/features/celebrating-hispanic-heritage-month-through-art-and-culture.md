## Celebrating Hispanic Culture Through Art and Music

Hispanic Heritage Month is an opportunity to recognize the histories, traditions, and contributions of Hispanic and Latino communities. Through art, music, and cultural expression, individuals and groups preserve their roots while introducing their traditions to new audiences.

From visual artists who express their perspectives through painting to musicians who keep cultural traditions alive through performance, Hispanic heritage takes many forms. Their work demonstrates how creativity can connect generations, celebrate identity, and keep cultural stories alive.

## Expressing Heritage Through Art

Art provides an opportunity for individuals to communicate their perspectives and share their appreciation for the world around them. Colombian painter Fabian Mira represents one of the artistic voices to highlight during Hispanic Heritage Month.

Fabian developed an interest in art during childhood and pursued his passion by studying art in Colombia. His paintings feature vibrant colors and elements of nature, including birds, deer, and landscapes.

Through his artistic work, Mira offers viewers a way to appreciate the natural world through his creative perspective. His journey also highlights the importance of supporting artists and recognizing the different ways creativity contributes to cultural expression.

## Preserving Afro-Puerto Rican Culture Through Music

Music is another powerful way communities preserve their heritage. Cimarrones, an Afro-Puerto Rican musical group founded by Alberto “Tito” Cepeda more than 15 years ago, brings together 26 members representing different age groups.

The group shares its culture through music, helping preserve its traditions so they are not forgotten. By bringing members of different generations together, Cimarrones creates a space where cultural knowledge and musical expression can continue to be shared.

Over the years, the group has traveled to numerous states across the United States and to Spain, introducing audiences in different places to its music and cultural heritage.

Its journey demonstrates how music can cross borders while maintaining a connection to the traditions and communities from which it originates.

## Keeping Cultural Traditions Alive for Future Generations

Although painting and music are different forms of expression, both offer ways to share experiences, communicate ideas, and connect with audiences.

Mira’s artwork highlights the role of visual creativity, while Cimarrones demonstrates how music can preserve Afro-Puerto Rican cultural traditions across generations. Together, their stories illustrate the diversity of artistic expression within Hispanic and Latino communities.

Hispanic Heritage Month is a reminder that cultural preservation depends on the people who continue to practice, share, and celebrate their traditions. By recognizing artists and cultural groups, communities can encourage younger generations to learn about their heritage and appreciate the many contributions that shape Hispanic culture.

Ultimately, celebrating Hispanic Heritage Month means recognizing not only the history of these communities, but also the people who keep their cultures alive today.
