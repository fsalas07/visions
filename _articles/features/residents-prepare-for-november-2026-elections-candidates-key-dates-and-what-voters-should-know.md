## Who Is Running?

### U.S. Senate

New Jersey voters will choose a candidate for the U.S. Senate, where the winner will serve a six-year term.

- **Cory Booker (Democrat):** The incumbent senator seeking reelection.
- **Justin Murphy (Republican):** The Republican nominee challenging Booker.

The Senate considers federal legislation, confirms presidential appointments and votes on matters involving the national budget and other federal responsibilities.

### U.S. House of Representatives — District 9

Paterson is part of New Jersey’s 9th Congressional District. Three candidates are listed for the November general election:

- **Nellie Pou (Democrat):** The incumbent representative seeking reelection.
- **Rosie Pino (Republican):** A Clifton councilwoman running for Congress.
- **Terrisa Bukovinac (Save Our Babies Party):** A third-party candidate on the ballot.

The race will determine who represents the district in the U.S. House of Representatives, where members vote on federal legislation, taxation, government spending and other national issues.

### Passaic County Commissioner

The county’s Board of County Commissioners oversees county government responsibilities, including the county budget and services.

Candidates reported for the November election include:

- **Orlando Cruz and Nick Veliky**, Democratic candidates seeking full terms.
- **Ed Leonard and John Rodriguez**, Republican candidates for the corresponding three-year seats.
- **Aleksandra Tasic**, the expected Democratic candidate for a separate unexpired-term seat.
- **Marko Kopec**, the Republican candidate for that unexpired term.

Because county candidate lists can change, voters should confirm the final certified names with the Passaic County Clerk before Election Day.

### Passaic County Surrogate

Another county race is for Surrogate, an office that handles certain matters involving wills, estates and related legal proceedings.

- **Zoila Cassanova (Democrat):** The incumbent seeking reelection.
- **Jessica Sprague (Republican):** An attorney challenging Cassanova.

These county races are separate from the congressional contest, but they also affect local government and services in the Paterson area.

## Important Dates for Paterson Voters

Residents planning to vote should keep these dates in mind:

- **October 13:** Voter registration deadline.
- **October 24–November 1:** In-person early voting.
- **October 27:** Deadline to apply for a mail-in ballot by mail.
- **November 3:** General Election Day. Polls are scheduled to be open from 6 a.m. to 8 p.m.

Eligible residents can register, check their registration status and find official voting information through [Vote.NJ.Gov](https://vote.nj.gov).

## How Can Residents Learn More?

Voters can research candidates’ public statements, policy proposals and records before making their decisions. They can also review the official sample ballot for their address to confirm which races will appear on their individual ballot.

For Paterson residents, it is important to remember that the city’s municipal elections take place in May, while November’s election includes state, federal and county contests. The offices and candidates on a ballot can vary according to a voter’s district and address.

## Why Participation Matters

Voting gives eligible residents a way to participate in choosing their representatives. Learning about candidates and understanding what each office does can help voters make informed decisions about the issues affecting their families and communities.

Young people can also become involved in civic life by following public debates, attending community meetings and learning how local, state and federal government work.

With November 3 approaching, Paterson residents are encouraged to verify their registration, review their ballots and consult official election resources before Election Day.
