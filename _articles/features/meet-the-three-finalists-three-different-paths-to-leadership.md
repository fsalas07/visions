## Meet the finalists

### Dr. Sanjay Rai

Rai has a background in mathematics and higher education administration. He earned a doctorate in mathematics from the University of Arkansas and held leadership positions at Montgomery College in Maryland before becoming Maryland’s secretary of higher education in 2024, following a period as acting secretary in 2023.

His experience includes working with state education policy and higher education institutions. He left the secretary position in March 2026; public announcements have not clearly established the reason for his departure.

### Dr. Chioma Ugochukwu

Ugochukwu brings experience in journalism, communication, and academic administration. She earned a doctorate in journalism from the University of Texas at Austin and has held leadership roles at institutions including Cottey College, Cabrini University, and Anna Maria College.

Her work has involved academic planning, accreditation, curriculum development, and student retention. Her curriculum vitae lists her appointment at Anna Maria College as ending in June 2026. The publicly available information reviewed does not establish the reason for that departure.

### Dr. Donald Guy Generals

Generals is a former PCCC president who later served as president of Community College of Philadelphia. He also held academic leadership roles at Mercer County Community College and earned an Ed.D. from Rutgers University.

During his leadership at Community College of Philadelphia, initiatives focused on scholarships, workforce development, and student support. In April 2025, the college’s board voted not to renew his contract and placed him on paid administrative leave. Generals sought legal assistance, and WHYY reported in May 2025 that a judge denied his request to return to the position. The reporting reviewed does not establish misconduct as the reason for the board’s decision.

## When can students and staff meet the candidates?

PCCC has scheduled three days of meetings on its main campus in Paterson, giving the college community an opportunity to hear directly from each finalist and ask questions.

The schedule is as follows:

- **Monday, October 26:** Dr. Sanjay Rai
- **Tuesday, October 27:** Dr. Chioma Ugochukwu
- **Wednesday, October 28:** Dr. Donald Guy Generals

Each day includes sessions for administrators and support staff from **10 to 11 a.m.**, an open community forum from **1:30 to 2:45 p.m.**, and a faculty session from **3 to 4 p.m.** The open forums are intended for students, faculty, staff, and members of the public. Online viewing is also available.

The forums give students an opportunity to learn about each candidate’s priorities, leadership style, and plans for the college’s future. Community feedback will also help inform the Board of Trustees as it considers the finalists.

## What happens next?

The selection of PCCC’s next president will shape the college’s direction as it continues serving students across Passaic County. While all three candidates have experience in higher education, their different professional paths may influence how they approach student success, academic programs, workforce partnerships, and institutional leadership.

The next step is for the college community to hear from the finalists before the selection process moves forward. Students and employees interested in participating should consult PCCC’s official presidential search page for the latest schedule, access details, and any registration instructions.
